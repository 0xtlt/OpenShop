---
title: Tutorials
description: Guided lessons that take you from a new project to a running Shopify integration.
---

Follow these lessons in order. Each one gives you a working result and tells you
what to look for before continuing. They assume basic TypeScript and terminal
experience; no previous OpenShop experience is needed.

| Lesson | What you will build | Before you start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. Build your first app](/tutorials/first-app/) | An embedded app that reads Shopify orders and calls a sample provider | Node.js 26, pnpm 11, PostgreSQL 17, Shopify CLI, and a development store |
| [2. Build a checkpointed flow](/tutorials/checkpointed-flow/) | A small job that pauses and resumes without repeating completed work | The running app from lesson 1 |

Already have an app and a specific task? Use the [how-to guides](/guides/).
To understand what you built, read [Building blocks](/concepts/building-blocks/).
